Our motif index is built by language models running on our own computer, with a person checking their work. Every change to how the models file and find stories is tried here first, on the person's own decisions, with its hypothesis written down before it runs, and kept only if it helps. Each experiment is named after a figure from folklore. The misses are here too: an idea that didn't work is a finding. The numbers are the experiments' own shorthand; the methods log tells the story of each change in plain words.

🪜 Each family of experiments is a ladder, from what's in use on the site at the top down to what was tried and not kept, colored by where each stands. Point at or tap an experiment for its question and what it found.

📊 The bar, where a result says a measure went from one number to another ({{ with_bar }} of {{ total }} do): grey is before, colored is after, from 0 to 100% (or 0 to 1, for a score like an AUC).
