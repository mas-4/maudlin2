📻 On the radio

The hourly radio newscasts of NPR (NPR News Now) and ABC News (ABC News Update), each story in the order it aired, beside what the news sites' front pages led with that hour. A local language model reads our own transcript of each newscast, splits it into its stories, writes a short headline for each in our words, and says which of that hour's front-page stories it is, if any. The number beside a story is its place on the front pages then: #1 is the story the most outlets had on their front pages. We keep the transcripts for this comparison and never publish them.
