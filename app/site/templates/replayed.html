📝 Read with care

  • The transcripts are machine-made (Whisper, on our own computer, or the show's own). A word can be wrong, and the quotes above are our transcript's words, matched in lowercase without punctuation and capitalized again for reading.
  • Playing a clip isn't agreeing with it. A show may play it to report it, to argue with it or to mock it; the episode tells which.
  • Times are the feeds' publish times, not when a show went out live. The time in the episode is where our transcript has it; ads put in by the podcast host can move between downloads.
  • It can miss things. A clip cut short or heard differently on a show won't match there, and we only read the shows we follow (the full list is on Shows and newsletters), as many of their episodes as our computer gets through.
  • Sorting is by rules that can be read off the words: a sponsor read says things like “promo code”, “terms apply” or a web address; a political ad says “paid for by”.{% if glitches %} We left out {{ glitches }} transcription glitches (the transcriber repeating a phrase, “you know you know…”).{% endif %}
