📺 On TV

What CNN, Fox News, MSNOW and BBC News kept on screen: the captions (chyrons) along the bottom of the picture, and for how long, beside what the news sites' front pages led with. The words come from the Internet Archive's TV News Archive, which reads the screen about once a minute (its Third Eye service). A local language model corrects the reading errors and sets aside names, promos and ads, and each headline is matched by meaning to the story on the front pages it's about. The number beside a story is its place on the front pages then: #1 is the story the most outlets had.
