📏 The divide, week by week

how different the stories left and right shows tell are

Each week (Tuesday to Monday) we compare the mix of motifs the hosts and guests of left-leaning shows said with the mix on right-leaning shows, and take away how different the same shows look when split into two sides at random. 0 means no more different than shows picked at random. Weeks only: a single day has too few tellings to tell apart from chance.
