🛠️ How it works

The puzzles are picked when the site is built, the same five for everyone all day. Two small word models help pick them, each trained on the three weeks of headlines before (TF-IDF and logistic regression, after we strip what our scraper picks up besides the headline, like bylines and "3 hours ago"). The satire model tells the satire feeds' titles from front-page headlines; the round uses the ones it gets wrong. The lean model guesses an outlet's rating from a headline's words: about 63% right on headlines it hasn't seen, against 41% for always guessing "right", and about 9 in 10 right when it's very sure. It reads house style as much as politics.

Your answers stay in your browser. The score counts both kinds of right answer, so calling everything satire scores no better than trusting everything. More in the methods log.
