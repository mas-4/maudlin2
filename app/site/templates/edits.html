Headline changes

Headlines outlets changed on articles they had already published, in the last {{ window_days }} days, newest first. Removed words are struck through and added words highlighted.

How this works

Each change is tagged with where it was seen:

  • feed: the outlet's RSS feed, whose titles are normally the article's own headline, so the article was most likely rewritten.
  • front page: the link text on the outlet's homepage. Outlets often use a shorter or different headline there, and change it when a story moves, so the article itself may still carry its original headline.

A change counts only if the old headline stopped appearing before the new one appeared, so the same article placed twice on a page isn't counted, and live blogs and reused links are skipped. {{ minor_count }} minor changes (capitalization, punctuation, an added or dropped label like "WEEKEND:", or a shortened version) aren't shown.
