🚧 Work in progress: how to read the map

The motif index drawn as a map: {{ motifs }} motifs a person has checked, each a dot that grows with its claims. Lines join a motif to the ones it rests on (it only makes sense given them: a kind of them, or a case, argument or figure that tells them), to motifs a person marked related, and to motifs that share a claim{% if groups %}; the colored areas are groups a person made of motifs that go together{% endif %}{% if genres %}, and a dot's color is its genre{% endif %}. Pick a dot to read its claims; drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom; more names show as you zoom in.
