🗺️ The outlet map

Every outlet we read on enough of our stories, placed by which stories it covers: outlets close together run the same stories. Each dot is in the outlet's AllSides lean color, white when it isn't rated. The second view places them by how they word them: how close their stories and words are to the AP's and Reuters', and how heated their headlines are.

💡 The first axis isn't left against right. Across, it runs from business, tech and world news to the political fight, and the partisan outlets of both sides sit together at the fight's end: they cover the same fight. Left and right part on the second axis: crime and immigration stories at the bottom, stories critical of Trump at the top.

🏷️ Outlet types

Outlets sorted into types by what they do on their front pages: which stories they run, when, beside whom, how close to the wires, how their headlines are worded, how often they rewrite them, about fifty measures in all, and none of them a rating. Outlets sit on a continuum more than in islands, so a type is shown only when it holds together: when we leave out a fifth of the outlets at random and sort them again, it comes back at least three-quarters the same. Most of the mainstream's finer types don't, so mainstream outlets carry only the broad family. A type named for a side is shown only on outlets rated on that side or not rated.
