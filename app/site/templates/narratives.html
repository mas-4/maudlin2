How this works

Folklore travels as variants: one claim or story told by many unrelated people, each in their own words. Copypasta and coordinated posting travel as the same words. So posts saying the same thing in different words are grouped together (by meaning, with an embedding model), and each group is measured by how many different people posted it and how varied their wording is. Feeds, bots and accounts posting many times an hour are left out.

For each big group, our local language model reads a few versions and says what they share, whether it's retold or just a shared topic, its genre, its chapter of Thompson's Motif-Index (the folklorist's catalog of story elements), and who it casts as villain, victim and hero. A link to the day's news appears only when the model, choosing among the closest stories with "none" on offer, picks one.

People's posts are never shown here, only the model's summary of what they share; accounts are never identified. Raw posts are kept 30 days for this research and never published.
