👋 About bignews.day

Nobody's immune to a good story

We read the news the way a folklorist reads tales: what happened, and the shapes of the stories people tell about it, left and right alike.

{% include "share.html" %}

📰 What it is

Every hour we read the front pages of 145 news sites, from far left to far right, and group their headlines into stories: who's running each one, how prominently, in what mood, and for how long. We follow each story on through TV captions, radio newscasts, over a hundred podcasts and shows across the spectrum, what people retell on Bluesky and Mastodon, and what fact-checkers make of it.

🧩 What's different

Underneath the news is a smaller set of stories people keep telling: they're going to steal the election, the law is being used against enemies, the leader is too old. Folklorists have catalogued the shapes of folktales for a century; we do the same for what's said about politics and public life, in the BND Motif Index. Each motif names a shape, with the claims told in it, the motifs that answer it, and the shows on the left, center and right it came up on. Often both sides tell the same story about each other: see them across the aisle.

⚖️ What it isn't

  • Not a fact-checker. A motif describes how a story is told, never whether it's true. Where a fact-checker has looked into a claim, we link to their verdict.
  • Not on a side. We file what tellers say, not why we think a story spreads, and the same shape gets the same name whoever tells it. Claims carry no party labels; the shows and outlets are marked by their lean so you can see who tells what.
  • Not finished. A language model running on our own computer suggests motifs and files claims; a person checks every motif before it's shown here. Expect some loose fits, and tell us when you find one. How we test the machine is in the Lab.

🔐 What we collect from Bluesky and Mastodon

  • A sample, read for its stories. We regularly listen to Bluesky's public stream for a few minutes at a time, and read a few pages of Mastodon's public timeline from people who chose to be searchable. We look for the stories people retell, counted across many posts.
  • No names. We keep a post's text and time and a salted fingerprint of its author, enough to count how many people said something, never to say who. No handles, images or links' previews. Labeled posts are skipped, a post deleted while we listen is deleted here, and raw posts are dropped after {{ retention }} days.
  • Nothing republished. We never post anyone's words as our own. A few posts that were already widely seen are shown embedded from Bluesky itself, so if the author deletes one it disappears here too. Mastodon posts are never shown. Datasets of posts are never published.
