🧰 How the workbench works

Left: every motif, by group, with kinds nested under the motif they're a kind of. The chips at the top are views (singles, no note, drafts…). Click a motif to open it; Shift-click (or ⧉) opens it beside the first, to compare, and as many more as you like; a plain click opens in the outlined panel.

Middle: the open motif: rename it, write its note, mark it done, and work its claims. Click claims to select several (Shift-click for a run).

Right: the inbox: claims to check, one at a time with every motif they're in (✓ fits or ✕ take out each; Y all fit, → next, ← back), pairs that may be one motif, single motifs, claims not filed yet, empty motifs.

Drag anything onto anything (hold a moment on a phone): a claim onto a motif files or moves it; a claim onto another claim says they're the same claim told two ways (the kept one shows ≡ and its other wordings, and repeats in those words are filed as it); a motif onto a motif merges, nests or relates them; a motif onto a group moves it there, onto a genre (sort the tree 🎭 by genre) gives it that genre. Hover a target and its choices pop up; let go on one. The drops in the top bar start a new motif, say "no motif", or take a claim out.

Columns: drag the bars between the panes to make one wider (double-click a bar to reset). Hover a motif to see its note and claims.

Keys: / search · Ctrl+Z undo · Esc cancel a drag or close a menu.
